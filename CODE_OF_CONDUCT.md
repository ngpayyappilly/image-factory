# Code of Conduct

ImageFactory is a project for people who care about secure, reproducible infrastructure. We want contributing to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent, and ask questions before assuming mistakes.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and kind in code review, and accept review in the same spirit.
- Welcome newcomers. A first contribution deserves a clear answer, not a lecture.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests, and keep discussions technical.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention directed at a person.
- Anything else that would be unacceptable in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@ngpayyappilly](https://github.com/ngpayyappilly), through the [private reporting form](https://github.com/ngpayyappilly/image-factory/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code, and will explain the decision to the people involved.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
